QUESTION 2 ANSWER (5 Points) — Kubernetes Deployment & HPA Setup
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Complete initial deployment.yml manifest (v1/guestbook/deployment.yml):

apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: guestbook
  labels:
    app: guestbook 
spec:
  replicas: 1
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: guestbook
  strategy:
    rollingUpdate:
      maxSurge: 25%
      maxUnavailable: 25%
    type: RollingUpdate
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: guestbook
    spec:
      containers:
      - image: guestbook:v1
        imagePullPolicy: Always
        name: guestbook
        ports:
        - containerPort: 3000
          name: http
        resources:
          limits:
            cpu: 50m
          requests:
            cpu: 20m

Commands executed:
kubectl apply -f deployment.yml
kubectl autoscale deployment guestbook --cpu-percent=10 --min=1 --max=10
